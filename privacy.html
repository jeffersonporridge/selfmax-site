Self Max

Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

Self Max collects nothing. There are no accounts, no servers and no analytics. The app contains no networking code, so there is nowhere for your information to go. Everything it records stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Self Max does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth, and it does not create an account for you. No information is transmitted off your device to us or to anyone else.

Location

Self Max asks for location access so it can tell when you arrive at and leave your gym. This is how gym time is measured.

  • Your location is evaluated on your device by iOS. It is never sent to us.
  • The app stores only the coordinates and radius of the gym you chose, and the number of minutes you have logged there.
  • That information is written to a private storage area shared between Self Max and its own extensions. No other app can read it.
  • Self Max does not keep a history of where you have been, and it does not track you when you are away from your saved gym.

Screen Time and app selections

Self Max uses Apple's Family Controls and Managed Settings frameworks to block and unblock apps.

  • When you pick apps or categories to block, Apple gives Self Max opaque tokens rather than app names. The app cannot see which apps you selected, and neither can we.
  • Screen time totals shown in the app are produced by Apple's own reporting extension, which runs in a sandbox that cannot send data anywhere.
  • These selections and totals stay on your device.

Third parties

There are none. Self Max includes no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporters and no third-party code that gathers information about you.

Purchases

Self Max Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple handles the transaction, and we never see or receive your payment details. What Apple tells the app is limited to whether a valid subscription exists. Apple's handling of that purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting your information

Because everything Self Max records is stored on your device, you control all of it:

  • Reset the app. Settings inside Self Max includes a reset that clears your rules, saved gym and history.
  • Delete the app. Removing Self Max from your device deletes everything it stored, permanently.

We hold no copy of your information, so there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

Self Max is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. If Self Max is used with Apple's Screen Time parental controls, that arrangement is governed by Apple.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Self Max can be sent to selfmaxapp@gmail.com.
