Common questions

Why do my apps stay blocked after I have been to the gym?

Self Max needs Always location access to notice you arriving and leaving while it is closed. With While Using, it can only see your location when the app is open on screen. Check Settings › Self Max › Location.

Does Self Max work if I close the app?

Yes, with Always location access. iOS wakes Self Max when you cross the edge of your saved gym, so a whole visit is recorded without opening it.

How do I cancel Self Max Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name at the top, tap Subscriptions, choose Self Max and then Cancel Subscription. There is also a Manage Subscription link inside the app's Settings.

Where is my data kept?

On your device, and nowhere else. Self Max has no servers and no accounts. See the Privacy Policy.
