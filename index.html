Questions people ask first.

Can the lock be fooled?
Self Max counts gym time from your iPhone's location, not from a button you tap or a photo you send. The clock only runs while you're at the gym you saved. Changing which gym counts takes 24 hours to kick in, so you can't move your gym to your couch on a lazy night. Deleting or weakening a rule waits 30 minutes in Chill mode, or 24 hours in Hardcore.
What if I need my phone in an emergency?
Self Max only locks the apps you pick. Calls, texts, maps and everything else keep working unless you choose to block them, and Emergency SOS always works. There's no instant unlock button, on purpose.
Why a subscription?
Self Max has no ads, sells no data and runs no servers, so the subscription is how it gets paid for. The yearly plan works out to $10 a month, and the free plan never expires. If you're already paying for a gym you don't go to, Self Max is the part that gets you through the door.
What does the free download include?
One rule that blocks one app, where 30 minutes at the gym unlocks 60 minutes of it, in Chill mode. Pro adds unlimited rules, as many apps, categories and websites as you want, your own targets and tiers, custom days and hours, and Hardcore mode. Every download starts with 7 days of Pro free.
